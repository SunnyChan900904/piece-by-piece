# 🐷 Piece by Piece 一塊一塊

**A cozy farm jigsaw game with a secret.**

You are Dumpling, a little pig born in the middle of June. Grandma found you at the market and said you looked hungry. Ever since, she has fed you, pampered you, and loved you every single day. And she has promised you a very special treat in December.

Every month on Grandma's farm is a jigsaw puzzle. Finish one, and a new chapter of your story unlocks.

### ✨ Features
- **A six-month story:** eight puzzle chapters from June to December, each with its own narrated scene
- **Things to find:** Grandma hides things in every picture. Some of them mean more than they seem
- **Secrets:** finish the story to unlock memories from everyone on the farm, and Grandma's own story
- **Music and sound:** an original music-box score that changes as the story unfolds
- **Two languages:** English and Traditional Chinese (繁體中文)
- **Free play:** any scene, any number of pieces, or your own photo
- **Saves your progress** in your browser, with separate farmer accounts for each player

### 🎮 Play
**[Play now →](https://sunnychan900904.github.io/piece-by-piece/)**

Works in any modern browser, on desktop and mobile. Use a mouse or touch to drag pieces, the scroll wheel or a pinch to zoom, and right-drag to move around the table.

### ⚠️ Content note
It looks cute. It isn't entirely. The story contains dark themes and a horror twist, and it is not intended for young children.
